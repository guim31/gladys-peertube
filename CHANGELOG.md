## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added

- PeerTube in Gladys: a device for the instance (online, local videos, views of
  the local videos, users, instances followed) and one per channel followed,
  local or from another instance (live on air, live viewers, last video and its
  date, followers, videos).
- With an account (user name and password): a device with the unread
  notifications and the followers of your channels.
- Scene triggers: new video, live started, live ended (with its duration and
  peak of viewers), each filterable on a channel.
- Scene action: read the latest videos (a sentence for a message or a voice
  announcement, and the details), for all channels or one.
- A dashboard widget: lives on air, unread notifications, and the latest
  videos as a poster grid with their thumbnails.
